Agents that finish the right task, inside the lines you set.

AgentOnTrack is a control layer for AI coding agents. You set the goal and approve what matters. Your agent does the work. Independent checks decide when it is done.

From goal to evidence

  1. 1

    Goal

    You approve a goal with a scope, exclusions and acceptance checks. It gets a version number.

  2. 2

    Authorize

    Each action is checked against the current goal, task and policy before it runs. Consequential ones wait for you.

  3. 3

    Execute

    Your own agent does the work, under its own login and your own model plan.

  4. 4

    Observe

    The workspace is compared afterwards with what was authorized. Differences are recorded.

  5. 5

    Verify

    AgentOnTrack runs your checks itself. "Done" from the agent counts for nothing.

Measured, including the misses

On 22 held-out scenarios of fixed action traces with no model in the loop, AgentOnTrack stopped or caught 10 of 15 unauthorized actions: 8 prevented, 2 detected afterwards, 5 missed, with 0 false blocks.

From scenarios recorded under the product's earlier working name. Self-authored and self-run. No independent evaluator has reproduced this. It does not measure live task success.

What it will not do

  • It cannot see the network activity of a script the agent runs.
  • It is not a security boundary against a determined program running as you.
  • It does not guarantee savings, safety or correct results.
Read the threat model →

Your code stays on your machine

Code, prompts, transcripts and credentials stay local. Your agent keeps its own login. Accounts and sync are optional, and sync sends only aggregate counts you can inspect first.
