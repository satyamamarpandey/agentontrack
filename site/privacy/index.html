Privacy

Plain terms. Draft pending legal review, and not yet in force on any public service.

This website

A static site on GitHub Pages. We set no cookies and run no analytics. GitHub may keep its own server logs. Feedback opens a public GitHub issue only if you choose to submit it.

Local use

With no account, nothing leaves your machine. Source code, prompts, transcripts, commands and credentials stay local.

If you create an account

We store your GitHub ID or email, your display name, your session records, the devices you register, your consent choices, and an audit log of account events.

If you turn on sync

Only: a project label you choose, token totals, daily counts of actions, task status counts, and safety counts. The server refuses any other field. Your project label is sent as written, so choose one you are happy to store.

What we never collect

Provider passwords, subscription tokens or session cookies. Source code, prompts or transcripts. There is no third-party tracking.

Your choices

Analytics, diagnostics, benchmark contributions, case studies and marketing are separate opt-ins, all off by default. You can export or delete your account from the dashboard. Deleting removes your data from the live database right away.

Retention

Sync records are kept for 365 days, and sign-in sessions for 14 days.

Cookies

Only what sign-in needs. No advertising or analytics cookies.
